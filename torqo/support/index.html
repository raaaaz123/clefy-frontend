Frequently asked questions

I can't hear the engine.
Turn up the volume and turn off silent mode, then tap START and drag the gas pedal up.
How do I shift gears?
In the default Full auto clutch mode, use the gear lever on the right. Other clutch modes let you work the clutch yourself.
How do I start the aircraft engines?
They follow a cockpit start. The status line under the gauges tells you the next step.
How do I undo my tuning?
Open Tune (the wrench at the top) and tap Reset to Stock.
Do you collect my data?
No. See our privacy policy.
