Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Torqo is an engine sound simulator. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it. The short version: nothing leaves your device.

Data we collect

None. Torqo has no account or sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. The app does not use the network.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (language, chosen engine, tuning, clutch mode and similar preferences) are saved on your iPhone only. Deleting the app removes them.

Engine clips and Photos

When you tap Clip, Torqo records a short video of the app screen with the app's own sound using Apple's ReplayKit. The microphone stays off. The clip is only saved if you tap Save, which asks for permission to add it to your Photos library. Torqo can add clips but cannot read your photos.

Children

Torqo collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
