Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Torqo is an engine sound simulator. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it. The short version: we collect anonymous usage data to improve the app, and nothing that identifies you.

Data we collect

Torqo has no account or sign-in and no advertising. It uses PostHog, an analytics service hosted in the United States, to understand how the app is used. PostHog receives:

  • Usage events, such as which engine you start, opening the Garage, finishing a drag run, or viewing the Lifetime offer.
  • Session replays: low-rate screenshots of the app's own screens and taps, so we can see where people get stuck. Text fields are masked.
  • A random app install identifier created by Torqo, your device model, iOS version, app version, language and an approximate country derived from your IP address.
  • Whether you own Lifetime Access.

None of this is linked to your name, email or Apple ID. We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we never sell data. Data is kept for up to one year. To ask for deletion, email us.

Purchases

Lifetime Access is bought through Apple. Apple handles payment; we never see your card or Apple ID. The app only checks with Apple whether you own it.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (language, chosen engine, tuning, clutch mode and similar preferences) are saved on your iPhone only. Deleting the app removes them.

Engine clips and Photos

When you tap Clip, Torqo records a short video of the app screen with the app's own sound using Apple's ReplayKit. The microphone stays off. The clip is only saved if you tap Save, which asks for permission to add it to your Photos library. Torqo can add clips but cannot read your photos.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Torqo schedules a few reminders on your iPhone itself (for example after some days without a rev). They are created on the device, nothing is sent to us, and you can turn them off any time in iPhone Settings.

Children

Torqo does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The analytics described above are anonymous.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
