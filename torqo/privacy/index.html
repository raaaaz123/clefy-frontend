Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Torqo is an engine sound simulator. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how to delete it. The short version: there is no sign-in. The iPhone app has no advertising; the Android app shows optional video ads from Google AdMob (see below). To run online races and leaderboards we keep a driver profile (the name you pick and, if you choose to tell us, your gender), and we collect usage data to improve the app. We never sell data.

Your driver profile

Torqo has no account or sign-in. The first time you use it, the app creates a random driver ID on this device. During setup it asks for:

  • A driver name. Any nickname works. It is shown to other players on races and leaderboards. You can change it any time.
  • Your gender (Male, Female, Other, or Prefer not to say). It is never shown to other players or on your profile. We use it only in aggregate, to understand who plays Torqo and shape the app for them.

With your driver ID we also store your country (from your iPhone's region setting), your race results (times, the engine, gearbox, tuning and car you raced with, and a speed trace of each run so others can race your ghost), reputation points, wins and losses, and any players you report or block.

Push notifications

If you allow notifications, the app sends our server a push token, your language and time zone, so we can send a few reminders and race updates (for example when someone beats your time). Each message we send is logged so we never send too many. You can turn notifications off any time in iPhone Settings.

Usage analytics

We use PostHog, an analytics service hosted in the United States, to understand how the app is used. PostHog receives:

  • Usage events, such as which engine you start, opening the Garage, finishing a drag run, or viewing the Lifetime offer, plus your answers in setup (such as your gender and what you are into).
  • Session replays: low-rate screenshots of the app's own screens and taps, so we can see where people get stuck. On iPhone, text fields are masked.
  • A random app install identifier created by Torqo, your device model, iOS version, app version, language and an approximate country derived from your IP address.
  • Whether you own Lifetime Access.

On iPhone we do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we never sell or share data for advertising.

Ads on Android

The Android app shows rewarded video ads from Google AdMob, only when you tap to watch one (a video unlocks a Pro engine for 5 minutes). To serve and measure these ads, Google receives your device's advertising ID, your IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with the ad. Google acts as an independent controller of this data under its own policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent first, and you can change it any time in Setup under Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Where it is stored and for how long

Your driver profile, races and push data are stored on Torqo's own server in the EU (France). Analytics are processed by PostHog in the United States. Data travels over encrypted connections. Analytics are kept for up to one year; your driver profile is kept until you ask us to delete it.

Deleting your data

Email us from the app's Support page with your driver name and we will delete your driver profile, races and push data from our server and ask PostHog to delete your analytics. Setup also has an option to start over as a new driver.

Purchases

Lifetime Access is bought through Apple. Apple handles payment; we never see your card or Apple ID. RevenueCat checks with Apple whether you own it, linked only to the random app ID.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (language, chosen engine, tuning, clutch mode and similar preferences) are saved on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes them.

Engine clips and Photos

When you tap Clip, Torqo records a short video of the app screen with the app's own sound using Apple's ReplayKit. The microphone stays off. The clip is only saved if you tap Save, which asks for permission to add it to your Photos library. Torqo can add clips but cannot read your photos.

Children

Torqo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us a name, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
