Frequently asked questions

How do I get the best palm reading?
Use your dominant hand, palm up and open, in bright, even light. Fill the frame and keep the photo sharp. If Lunra can't see your lines clearly it will ask for another photo instead of guessing.
Are my photos stored?
No. A photo is sent only when you ask for a reading, read in memory and discarded right after. It is never saved on a server or used to train AI. See our privacy policy.
Why is my reading in another language?
Readings follow your iPhone's language. Change it in iPhone Settings > General > Language & Region.
How do I add the widget?
Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Lunra. It shows your card of the day and today's horoscope headline.
How do I change or stop reminders?
Open Lunra > Settings (gear icon) and turn the morning reminder or evening check-in off.
How do I cancel a subscription?
Lunra > Settings > Manage subscription, or iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions. Apple handles all payments and refunds.
How do I delete my data?
Lunra > Settings > Delete all data erases everything on your iPhone. We never keep your data, so there is nothing stored on our side.
