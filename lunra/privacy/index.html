Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

Lunra reads your palm, face or coffee cup, draws tarot cards, writes a daily horoscope and checks love compatibility. We built it so that we never keep your data.

The short version

  • No account and no sign in.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • We do not store your photos, your profile or your readings on any server.
  • We do not sell or share personal data, and we never use it to train AI.

What stays on your iPhone

Your first name, birthday and zodiac sign, the topics you chose, your relationship status and dominant hand, your saved readings and love matches, your mood check-ins, your streak and your settings are saved only in the app on your device. They are never sent to us to be stored. The home-screen widget reads your sign and today's card from the same on-device storage.

Reminders are local notifications that your iPhone schedules for itself. No push server is involved.

Select Settings > Delete all data to erase everything at any time. Deleting the app removes it too.

What is sent for a reading

Readings are written by AI. Only after you agree on the "Before your first reading" screen, and only when you ask for a reading, the following is sent over an encrypted connection:

  • Palm, face or coffee cup reading: the photo you took or chose, plus your first name, zodiac sign, age, chosen topics, relationship status and which hand it is. This personalizes the reading.
  • Love match: your first name and sign, short traits from your last palm reading, and your partner's first name and sign. If you add their palm photo, that photo is sent too.
  • Tarot: the cards you drew, your question if you typed one, and the same profile details as above.
  • Daily horoscope: your zodiac sign, today's date, your language and chosen topics. If you scanned your palm, a few short traits from that reading are also sent.

These go to:

  • Lunra's server, which passes the request on and returns your reading.
  • Amazon Bedrock, a service of Amazon Web Services (AWS), which runs the AI models (Kimi, GLM, Qwen) that write the reading.

Everything is processed in memory for that single request and then discarded. Photos are never written to disk. We do not log the content of requests. AWS states that Amazon Bedrock does not store prompts or outputs and does not use them to train models.

One exception holds no personal data. Generic daily horoscopes, keyed only by sign, date, language and topics, are kept in server memory for up to a day so everyone with the same sign gets them faster. Horoscopes that use your palm traits are never cached.

Like any internet service, our server briefly sees your IP address to deliver the response. To limit abuse, it keeps a short-lived, salted, one-way hash of it in memory. The real IP address is never stored or logged, and the hash disappears when the server restarts.

Your choice

You can turn online readings off at any time in Settings. When they are off, nothing leaves your iPhone. The card of the day, the moon phase, your mood check-ins and your saved readings still work.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe, Apple handles the payment. We never see your payment details. Manage or cancel any time in Settings > Manage subscription.

Children

Lunra is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
