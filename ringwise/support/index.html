Frequently asked questions

How do I get accurate punch counts?
Stand the iPhone still (a tripod or a shelf works), 2 to 3 metres away, with your whole body in view and good light in front of you. Holding the phone in your hand makes tracking much less reliable.
Does Ringwise need an internet connection?
Not for training. The round timer, combo caller and punch tracking work offline, and your rounds back up to your account when you are online again. Video coach reports, online fights and leaderboards need a connection.
Can I play music while training?
Yes. The round bell and combo calls mix with your music instead of stopping it.
How do I turn off the video coach upload?
Open Settings > Video coach. Ringwise always asks before the first upload.
How do I change or stop practice reminders?
Open Settings > Reminders and pick your days, or turn notifications off in iPhone Settings.
How do I move to a new iPhone?
Settings > Sign in with Apple links your account. On the new iPhone, tap "I already have an account" and sign in with the same Apple ID. Your profile, rounds, reports and fight record come back; videos stay on the old iPhone.
How do I delete my account?
Settings > Delete Account and Data permanently deletes your account and everything saved in it on our server, plus every video and report on your iPhone. Backups that still contain it are removed within 14 days. You can also email us to delete it.
