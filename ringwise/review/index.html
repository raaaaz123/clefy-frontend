How to test Ringwise

No account or sign-in. Everything a user records, their profile and their reports are saved only on the iPhone. We do not collect or store any user data.

1. Live punch tracking (Train tab)
Needs a person boxing in front of the camera. No boxer available? Play the shadow boxing clip below full screen on a computer or TV, stand the iPhone 1.5 to 2 m away so the whole body on the screen is in view, then tap Start round. Punches are counted, named and called out live; the round summary opens at the end.
2. Video coach (Analyze > My training)
Save the shadow boxing clip to Photos (open it, Share, Save Video). In Ringwise: Analyze, My training, Library, pick the clip, skip the filming tips, tap Agree to the one-time notice, then tap the boxer. The report (score, weak spots on the frame, fixes with drills, punch stats) is ready in about a minute. Needs internet.
3. Scout an opponent (Analyze > Scout opponent)
Save the sparring clip to Photos. Analyze, Scout opponent, Library, pick it, and tap the fighter in the red shirt. The report shows their openings on the frame, habits, threats and a game plan.
What leaves the phone
Only for a video report, and only after the user agrees: up to 16 still frames and the measured numbers go to our server, which asks Amazon Bedrock to write the report. Nothing is stored or logged. Live tracking never leaves the iPhone.
