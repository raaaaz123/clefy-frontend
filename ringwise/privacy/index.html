Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Ringwise keeps your training in your Ringwise account so it is safe and you can fight other boxers online. There are no ads and no third-party tracking.

Your Ringwise account

When you finish setup, Ringwise creates an account for you on the Ringwise server. You can link it to your Apple ID with Sign in with Apple, so you can sign in again on another iPhone. With Sign in with Apple, we receive a private identifier from Apple and, if you share it, your name. We never see your Apple password, and we do not ask for your email address.

The account holds: - Your profile: the name you enter, level, goal, stance, gear, country (from your iPhone's region) and app settings. - Your training: the numbers from each round (date, length, punch counts by type, how much of the round your body was in view). - Your coach reports: the text and scores of each video report, and the numbers measured from that clip. - Your fights: results, scores, health left, your result on each pad and incoming punch, XP, level and rating.

Leaderboards and online fights

Your display name, country flag, level, XP, rating and win-loss record are shown to other Ringwise users on leaderboards and when you are matched in a fight. In a live 1v1 or a ghost fight, your opponent sees your name, flag, level, rating and your result on each pad as it happens. No camera image, video or body tracking is ever shared with other users. You can report or block a boxer.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Camera. Body tracking runs on your iPhone. Live camera frames never leave it, in training or in fights.
  • Videos. Recorded rounds and videos you analyze are kept only on your iPhone, with their tracked joints.

Video reports

When you request a video report (Analyze), Ringwise sends up to 16 still frames from that clip and the numbers it measured to the Ringwise server. The server passes them to Amazon Bedrock (Amazon Web Services), where an AI model writes your report. The frames are used for that one request and are not stored. The finished report is saved to your account. Amazon Bedrock does not use your data to train models. You can turn this off in Settings > Video coach.

What we never do

  • No advertising and no third-party analytics.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No access to your photo library beyond the video you choose.

Where data is kept

The Ringwise server runs in a data centre in the European Union. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). A nightly backup is kept for 14 days.

Children

Ringwise is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Settings > Delete Account and Data permanently deletes your account and everything saved in it on the server, and every video and report on your iPhone. Backups that still contain it are removed within 14 days. Deleting the app alone does not delete your account; use the button first, or email us.
