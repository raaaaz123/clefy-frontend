Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Ringwise is built so your training stays yours. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Camera. Ringwise uses the camera to see your punches. The body tracking runs on your iPhone. Live frames are never sent anywhere.
  • Your rounds. Recorded rounds (video, tracked joints, punch counts and your labels) are saved only on your iPhone.
  • Your profile. Your name, level, goal, stance and gear are saved only on your iPhone.
  • Videos you analyze. A video you record or pick from your library is copied into the app and tracked on your iPhone.

What leaves your iPhone, and only when you ask

When you request a video report (Analyze), Ringwise sends up to 16 still frames from that clip and the numbers it measured (punch counts, guard, pace, your level, goal and stance) to the Ringwise server. The server passes them to Amazon Bedrock (Amazon Web Services), where an AI model writes your report. The report comes back to your iPhone.

  • Nothing is stored on the Ringwise server. Frames and numbers are used in memory for that one request and are not logged.
  • Amazon Bedrock does not use your data to train models.
  • To limit abuse, the server counts requests per device using a one-way hash of your IP address that is kept in memory only and never written down.
  • You can turn this off in Settings > Video coach. Ringwise asks before the first upload.

What we never do

  • No accounts, sign-in, advertising or third-party analytics.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.
  • No access to your photo library beyond the video you choose.

Children

Ringwise is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Settings > Delete all data removes every round, video, report and your profile from your iPhone. Deleting the app does the same.
