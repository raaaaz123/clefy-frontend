Frequently asked questions

How accurate is the AI detector?
No detector is perfect. In our tests it caught about 84% of AI text and flagged about 5% of human text. Treat the score as an estimate, never as proof.
Is my text stored?
No. Text is sent only when you tap a feature, processed, then discarded. It is never stored or used to train AI. See our privacy policy.
Why does Check text say "basic estimate"?
AI processing is off. Turn it on in Settings > AI processing for the more accurate check.
How do I use Veriwrite from other apps?
Select text in any app, tap Share, then Writo. Tap Save for Humanize and it will be waiting the next time you open the app.
How do I delete my history?
Settings > Clear history. Your name, My Voice samples and history live only on your iPhone, so deleting the app removes everything.
