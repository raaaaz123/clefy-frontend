Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

Writo helps you check whether text was written by AI and rewrite it so it sounds natural. We built it to collect as little as possible.

No account, no tracking

Writo has no sign in. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we do not sell or share personal data.

What stays in the app

Your first name, your onboarding answers (role, goals, writing voice), your My Voice writing samples and your history are saved only in the app on your device. They are never sent to us. You can delete history in Settings at any time. Deleting the app removes everything.

Text you choose to analyze

Only after you allow AI processing, and only when you tap a feature, the text you used it on is sent over an encrypted connection to:

  • Writo's server, which estimates whether the text is AI-written using an open-source detection model, and reads the text out of Word files you import.
  • Amazon Bedrock, a service of Amazon Web Services (AWS), which runs the AI models (GLM, Kimi, Qwen) used by Humanize, the editing tools (shorter, longer, tone, grammar), Fix flagged sentences and Turn into writing. If you turn on My Voice, your writing samples are sent with these requests so the rewrite matches your style.
  • Amazon Polly (AWS), which turns text into speech when you tap Listen.
  • OpenAlex and Wikipedia (public search services), which receive a few of your most distinctive sentences as search queries when you tap Check for matching sources. These searches are sent from our server, not from your device.

Speak your ideas uses Apple speech recognition, which runs on your device when supported. Only the resulting text is sent, when you tap Turn into writing.

The text is used only to return your result. It is processed in memory and then discarded: we do not store or log it, and it is not used to train AI models. AWS states that Amazon Bedrock does not store prompts or outputs and does not use them to train models.

No other information (such as your name or history) is sent with the text. Our server temporarily sees your IP address, as any internet service does, to deliver the response and limit abuse. It is not stored.

Your choice

You can turn AI processing off at any time in Settings. When it is off, nothing is sent: Check text gives a basic estimate made on your device and the other AI features are unavailable.

Children

Writo is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.
