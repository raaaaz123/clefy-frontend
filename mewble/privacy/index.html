Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

Mewble ("the app") is a virtual pet that lives on your iPhone. The short version: Mewble does not collect any data. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything stays on your iPhone.

Data we collect

None. Mewble has no servers and does not send your data anywhere. We (the developer) can't see your pet, your habits or anything you do in the app.

What stays on your iPhone

To work, the app saves these things on your device only:

  • Your pets, their names, looks, room and decorations
  • Your habits, check-ins, focus sessions, walks and sleep times
  • Your settings (sounds, haptics, reminders, bedtime)

Widgets, Live Activities and the Dynamic Island read the same data from your iPhone. If your iPhone is backed up to iCloud or a computer, this data is included in that backup, which is handled by Apple.

Permissions

Mewble only asks when you use a feature that needs it, and you can say no:

  • Motion & Fitness: counts your steps on this iPhone during Walk with me. Steps are never uploaded.
  • Camera: shows your pet on your real desk in AR. Nothing is recorded or saved.
  • Photos: when you make a coat from a photo, you pick one picture and its colours are read on your iPhone. The photo is not stored or uploaded. Saving a picture of your pet only adds it to your library; the app can't read your other photos.
  • Notifications: reminders and bedtime nudges are scheduled on your iPhone. No notification data is sent to us.

Deleting your data

  • Me > Delete all data permanently erases your pets, habits, history and settings from this iPhone and starts over.
  • Deleting the app also removes all of its data.
  • Because we never receive your data, there is nothing stored on our side to delete.

Children

Mewble is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
