Frequently asked questions

How do I put my pet on my Home Screen or Lock Screen?
Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Mewble. In the app, Me > Put your pet on your Home Screen shows the steps.
How do I see my pet in the Dynamic Island?
Start a focus session, a walk or bedtime. Your pet appears in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen while it runs. iPhones without a Dynamic Island show it on the Lock Screen.
Can my pet walk around on top of other apps?
No. iOS doesn't allow any app to draw over other apps. Your pet lives in Mewble, widgets, Live Activities and the Dynamic Island.
Steps aren't counting.
Allow Motion & Fitness for Mewble in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Motion & Fitness.
How do I move my pet to a new iPhone?
Your pet is part of your iPhone backup, so it moves with iCloud backup or Quick Start. You can also use Me > Export a backup.
How do I delete my data?
Open Me > Delete all data. It erases everything on your iPhone and starts over. We never receive your data, so there is nothing stored on our side. See our privacy policy.
