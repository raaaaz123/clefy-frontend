Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

Clefy ("the app") collects only what it needs to save your progress and to make the app better. We never sell your data, show ads or track you across other apps.

What we collect

Your learning progress. So you never lose your streak, the app backs up your progress to Clefy's own server:

  • Onboarding answers (instrument, goal, level, daily goal, reminder time)
  • Lessons completed, XP, streak, practice history and which notes and skills you find hard
  • A random ID created for this device, so your progress is linked to you without a sign-up

Sign in with Apple (optional). If you choose "Save your progress", Apple gives us an ID for your account and, if you allow it, your name and email (which may be Apple's private relay address). We use these only to restore your progress on a new iPhone.

Usage analytics. We use PostHog to understand how the app is used: which screens and lessons people open, where they get stuck, and crash reports. This includes session replays, which record taps and screen layouts (text fields are masked). Analytics are linked to your random app ID, not to your name.

Subscriptions. If you start a Clefy Plus free trial or subscription, Apple handles the payment. We use RevenueCat to check that your subscription is active, so it receives your purchase history (which plan, when it started or renews) linked to your random app ID. We never see your card or payment details.

Question reports. If you tap the flag on a question, we receive that question and your answer so we can fix it.

What we don't collect

  • Microphone audio. The Tuner listens to work out the pitch on your iPhone. Sound is never recorded, saved or uploaded.
  • Contacts, photos, location, advertising IDs or card and payment details.

Where it is stored

Progress is stored on Clefy's server in the EU (France). Analytics are processed by PostHog and subscription status by RevenueCat, both in the United States. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

Deleting your data

  • Profile > Settings > Delete account permanently deletes your account and all progress from our server and your iPhone.
  • Profile > Settings > Clear data and start over erases your progress on the server and on this iPhone but keeps the app installed.
  • You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone. No notification data is sent to us.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 beyond the anonymous progress and usage data described above. Sign in with Apple is optional.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
