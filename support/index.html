Frequently asked questions

I can't hear any sound.
Turn up the volume and check Profile > Sound is on. Sound plays even in silent mode.
Can I move my progress to a new phone?
Progress lives on your device and moves with an iPhone backup or Quick Start transfer.
How do I start over?
Open Profile > Reset progress. This can't be undone.
How do I change my reminder?
Open Profile > Reminder and pick a time, or turn notifications off in iPhone Settings.
Do you collect my data?
No. See our privacy policy.
